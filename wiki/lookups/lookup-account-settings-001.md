# Where to look in any account
Every company lays out its settings differently, but most use similar names for the same sections. These are the sections to open on any account you care about.

## Rows

### Privacy
Privacy & Safety · Privacy and security · Audience
Who can see what you post, and who can contact you.

### Ads
Ad preferences · Personalisation · Interest-based ads
Whether the ads you see are chosen from what you do on that service and on other sites and apps.

### Data
Your data · Data sharing · Download your information
Whether the company shares your data with other companies, and how to download a copy of what it holds about you.

### Activity
History · Activity log · My activity
What is saved about your activity, and whether it is deleted automatically after a set time.

### Connected apps
Apps and websites · Third-party apps · Linked accounts
Other apps and websites you have allowed into this account, including ones you may no longer use. You can remove their access here.

### Visibility
Who can find me · Discoverability · Search settings
Whether people can find you by your phone number or email address.

### Security
Login & security · Sign-in · Two-step verification
Two-factor authentication, and which devices are signed in to the account.

## Notes
- Check these sections again from time to time, because companies add new settings and move old ones when they redesign their apps.

## Verify yourself
Open the settings on an account you already have. You should recognise most of these words.
