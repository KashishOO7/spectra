


## who
### opportunistic
Automated bots & scammers
Scam messages sent to millions, stolen passwords tried in bulk, harmful software left lying around. Not aimed at you, aimed at whoever answers.
### data_broker
Data brokers
Companies that collect and sell personal details, usually ones you have never heard of.
### criminal_org
Organised criminals
Groups that lock up your files and demand money, and fraud rings with real resources behind them.
### ai_automated
AI-powered attacks
Scam messages written for you personally, copied voices, faked video. Automated, and increasingly convincing.
### targeted_individual
Someone targeting me
One particular person who has decided to come after you, whether skilled or simply persistent.
### intimate_partner
A current or former partner
Someone with physical access, relationship trust, and possibly your passwords already.
### employer
My employer or school
Monitoring of work accounts, and management software on any device your employer or school controls.
### isp_network
My internet provider
The company providing your internet, or anyone else able to watch that connection.
### domestic_government
My own government
Law enforcement or intelligence agencies in your country.
### foreign_government
A foreign government
Foreign intelligence services. Very capable, and they choose their targets.

## situations


### caring_for_someone
I look after someone else's safety online
A child, a parent, anyone in your care.
### known_person_risk
Someone I know may be a risk to me
An ex or someone close who follows where you go, watches your devices, shares images, or will not leave you alone.
### public_work
My work or profile draws attention
Reporting, campaigning, research, or simply being visible enough to be worth targeting.
### work_accounts
I use work devices or company accounts
Work laptops, company logins, and the data that sits behind them.
### ai_focused
I want deep coverage of AI threats
AI-written scams, copied voices, faked video, and AI used to gather what is already public about you.
