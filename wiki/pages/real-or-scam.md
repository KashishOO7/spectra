## messages

### msg-urgency
Text message from an unknown number
Unknown number
Your parcel is on hold. Pay the 1.99 redelivery fee within 12 hours or it goes back to the sender: parcel-redelivery-help.com
The message sets a short deadline and asks for a small fee, and it never says which delivery company sent it, which a real delivery message does. The deadline is there to make you pay before you check. To check a delivery, open the delivery company's own app or website yourself.

### msg-authority
Email from "IT Support"
IT Support
Your mailbox is almost full and will be switched off today. Confirm your password at mailbox-verify-portal.com to keep your account.
The email uses the name of someone in charge, so that you follow the instruction without asking questions. The link goes to a website that is not your email provider's, and a password you type there goes to whoever sent the email. To see whether your mailbox is really full, open your email settings yourself.

### msg-trust
Message from an unknown number
Unknown number
Hi Mum, I dropped my phone and this is my new number. Can you send 400 for a bill today? I'll pay you back tomorrow.
The message says it is from your child, but it comes from a number you have never seen, and nothing in it shows who really sent it. Before you send anything, call your child on the number you already have for them.

### msg-fear
Text message from "Your Bank"
Your Bank
We have stopped a payment of 950 from your account. If this was not you, call us now on 555 0199 to protect your money.
The text tells you about a payment you did not make, to frighten you, and gives you a number to call. If you call that number, you speak to whoever sent the text, not to your bank. Call the number on the back of your card, or open your bank's app, instead.

### msg-scarcity
Advert in your social media feed
Designer Outlet Deals
Only 3 left at 80% off. The offer ends at midnight. Order now at designer-outlet-deals.shop
The advert says the offer will run out, so that you pay before you look closely at the website. A price far below what other websites charge, on a website you do not know, is a reason to stop and check that website first.

### msg-reciprocity
Email from someone you met once
Jordan Ellis
It was great to meet you last week. As a thank you, here is a gift card. Sign in with your email account to redeem it: giftcard-redeem-now.com
The email gives you a gift first, so that you feel you owe the sender something and click the link. The link asks you to sign in on a website that is not your email provider's, and that is how the sender gets your password.

### msg-social
Message in a neighbourhood group chat
Neighbourhood group
2,000 people in our area already earn 300 a week from this app. I did too. Join before the places run out: earn-daily-tasks.app
The message says many people near you already earn money this way, so joining feels safe. The number of people is only what the message says, and the account that posted it may not belong to the person you think.

### real-code
Text message, just after you asked to sign in
Your account
Your sign-in code is 482913. Do not share it with anyone. We will never call to ask for it.
You asked for this code yourself a moment ago, the message has no link and asks you for nothing, and it tells you not to share the code. If a code arrives that you did not ask for, never share it with anyone.

### real-delivery
Text message from your delivery company's usual number
Parcel delivery
Your parcel will arrive this afternoon. You can track it in our app.
The message asks for no money and no details, and it sends you to the company's own app rather than a link. You can check it by opening that app yourself.

### real-friend
Message from your friend's usual number
Sam
Running 10 minutes late, sorry! Save me a seat.
The message comes from the number you already have for your friend, and it asks you for nothing.

### real-bank
Notification in your bank's own app
Your bank
You paid 12.40 at the supermarket. Tap to see the details.
The message is a notification from the bank's own app on your phone, not a text or an email, and it only tells you about a payment you made. If you ever see a payment you did not make, contact the bank through its app or the number on your card.
