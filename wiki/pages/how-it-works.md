How Spectra works | Spectra

## description
Spectra in about a minute: a short list of plain safety steps in order, you do one, and it remembers where you got to. Say what is going on and the order changes.

## heading
How Spectra works

## lead
Spectra is a checklist that puts itself in order for you.

## reading-time
About a minute to read.

## steps
### You say what you would rather avoid
The front page lists plain things people would rather did not happen to them. Tap the ones that sound like you. Tap none of them and press the button anyway, which also works, and change your mind whenever you like.

### You get a short list, in order
Spectra puts the same set of steps in the order that fits what you tapped. You see one at a time rather than a wall of everything at once.

### You do one thing
Each step is a single action in plain words, with the setting named for your phone or your computer. If a step does not apply to you, say so and it moves out of your way.

### It remembers where you got to
What you have done is saved in this browser, so you can stop, close the tab, and pick it up later from the same place.

## why-heading
Why the order changes

## why
Your answers change the order of your playbook, and some situations add steps written for them. Somebody worried about a person they know needs different steps first from somebody worried about scams sent to millions of people at once, and gets some steps that only apply to them. Spectra does that for you.

You never have to take the order on trust. Every step says why it is there, and the page under the hood shows exactly how the ordering is worked out, including what it is not good at.

## limits-heading
What Spectra does not do

## limits
### It never looks at your accounts, files or devices
Spectra has no way to see any of them. Nothing it shows you is based on checking anything of yours.

### What you tap never leaves this browser
What you tap stays in this browser, on this device. There is no account and nothing to sign up for. The page loads its fonts from Google's servers, and nothing you tap goes with that request.

### Spectra does not tell you what will happen to you
Each step says what is generally worth doing and links the source it came from, so you can read the reasoning and decide for yourself.
