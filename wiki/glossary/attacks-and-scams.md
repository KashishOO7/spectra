
## attack vector
also: attack vectors

The way an attacker gets to your accounts, devices or money, such as a phishing message, a reused password or a flaw in an app.

## phishing

A message made to look as if it comes from someone you trust, such as your bank, to get you to type in a password or card number, or to open a harmful link or file.

## social engineering

Tricking a person into giving away information, access or money, instead of breaking into the technology.

## ransomware

Malware that encrypts the files on a device so they cannot be opened. The criminals behind it then demand payment to unlock them.

## malware

Any software made to cause harm on a device, such as stealing data, spying on its owner or locking its files.

## stalkerware

An app or program put on someone's phone without their knowledge or consent, which lets another person secretly see their location, messages, photos, calls and searches.

## voice cloning
also: deepfake, deepfakes

Using AI to make audio or video that copies a real person's voice or face, from recordings of them. It can be convincing enough to fool people who know them.

## AI language model
also: language model, large language model, LLM

An AI trained on very large amounts of text, so that it can write, summarise and translate text. AI chat apps are built on this kind of AI.

## exploit
also: exploited, exploiting

A method or piece of code that uses a flaw in software to make it do something its makers never intended, such as letting an attacker in.

## reverse image search
also: reverse image searches

Searching the web with a picture instead of words, to find other websites where the same picture, or one like it, appears.

## cryptocurrency
also: crypto

Digital money that is usually not issued by a bank or a government, kept in a digital wallet and sent from one wallet to another. Once you pay with it, you can usually get the money back only if the person you paid sends it back.

## remote access app
also: remote access apps

An app that lets someone in another place see your screen and control your computer or phone over the internet, as if they were sitting at it.

## grooming

An adult building a child's trust step by step, often with attention, gifts and secrets, so that they can abuse the child.
