


## two-factor authentication
also: 2FA, two-step login, two-step verification, second factor

A login that asks for a second proof after your password, usually a code or an approval on your phone.

## credentials
also: credential

The username and password you use to sign in to an account.

## password manager

An app that creates a different password for every account and saves them for you.

## authenticator app
also: authenticator

An app on your phone that shows a login code to type in after your password. The code keeps changing, and the app works without an internet connection or a phone signal.

## backup codes
also: backup code, recovery codes

A set of codes you can get from an account once two-factor authentication is on. Each code works once, in place of the code from your phone, if you lose the phone or cannot get codes on it.

## passphrase

A password made of several words chosen at random. Its length makes it hard to guess, and real words are easier to remember than random letters and symbols.

## credential stuffing
also: credential-stuffing

Attackers take usernames and passwords stolen from one site and use a program to try them on many other sites, looking for people who reused the same password.

## SIM swap
also: SIM-swapping, SIM swapping

Someone tricks your mobile provider into giving them your phone number, so your calls and texts, including login codes, go to their phone instead of yours.

## passkey
also: passkeys

A sign-in key your device creates for one site and unlocks with your fingerprint, face or screen lock, in place of a password.

## sign-in prompt
also: sign-in prompts

A message an account sends to your signed-in phone, asking you to approve or refuse a sign-in.

## account takeover

Someone else gets into your account and uses it as you. They may also change the password or recovery details so that you cannot get back in.
