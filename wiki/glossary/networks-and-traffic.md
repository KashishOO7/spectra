
## end-to-end encryption
also: E2EE, end to end

Messages are encrypted on your device and can be decrypted only on the device of the person you send them to, so no one in between can read them, including the company that runs the app.

## DNS
also: domain name system

The system that turns a website's name, such as example.com, into the numeric address your device uses to connect to it.

## VPN

A service that encrypts your internet traffic between your device and a server run by the VPN provider. The network you are on and your internet provider cannot see where your traffic goes, but the VPN provider can.

## internet provider
also: ISP

The company that provides your internet connection, whether over Wi-Fi or the mobile network.

## public wifi

A Wi-Fi network in a public place, such as a café, hotel or airport, that anyone nearby can join.

## SMS
also: SMS text messages

Text messages sent through your mobile network rather than through an app. They are not encrypted.

## SS7

The system telephone networks use to set up calls and pass text messages between each other. Known weaknesses in it can let someone with access to it intercept calls and text messages, including login codes.
