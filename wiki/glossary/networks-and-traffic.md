
## end-to-end encryption
also: E2EE, end to end

Messages are encrypted on your device and can be decrypted only on your devices and those of the people you send them to, so no one in between can read them on the way, including the company that runs the app.

## DNS
also: domain name system

The system that turns a website's name, such as example.com, into the numeric address your device uses to connect to it.

## VPN

A service that encrypts your internet traffic between your device and a server run by the VPN provider. The network you are on and your internet provider cannot see where your traffic goes, but the VPN provider can.

## internet provider
also: ISP

The company that provides your internet connection, whether over Wi-Fi or the mobile network.

## public wifi

A Wi-Fi network in a public place, such as a café, hotel or airport, that anyone nearby can join, sometimes with a password given to every customer.

## SMS
also: SMS text messages

Text messages that travel through your mobile provider's network rather than over the internet. They are not end-to-end encrypted, so your mobile provider can read them.

## SS7

The system telephone networks use to set up calls and pass text messages between each other. Known weaknesses in it can let someone with access to it intercept calls and text messages, including login codes.

## router

The box that runs your Wi-Fi and connects your home to the internet, sometimes together with a separate modem.

## guest network

A second Wi-Fi network on your router, with its own name and password, kept apart from your main network on most routers, so devices on it cannot easily reach your computers and phones.

## WPA3
also: WPA2

The security standards that encrypt your Wi-Fi, so people nearby who do not have the password cannot read what your devices send over it. WPA3 is the newer one.

## WPS

A router feature that lets a device join your Wi-Fi when someone presses a button on the router, without typing the password.

## UPnP

A router feature that lets devices on your network open the router to connections from the internet without asking you.
