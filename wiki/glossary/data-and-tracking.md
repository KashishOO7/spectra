
## data breach
also: breach, breaches, data breaches

Personal records held by a company or organisation are stolen or exposed, so that people who should not have them get copies.

## session token
also: session tokens

A code your browser keeps after you sign in and sends back on each visit, so the site does not ask for your password again. Anyone who copies it can use your account as you, without your password.

## data broker
also: data brokers

A company that collects personal details about people, such as their address and phone number, from public records, websites and other companies, and sells them.

## people-search site
also: people-search sites, people search site, people search sites

A website where anyone can type in a name and see that person's address, phone number, age and relatives' names. It is one kind of data broker.

## credit freeze
also: credit freezes

A block on your credit file, so no one can open a loan or card in your name until you lift it.

## opt-out
also: opt-outs, opt out

A request asking a company to remove your details or stop using them. On a people-search site it is the form that removes your page.

## metadata

Information about a message or call rather than what it says: who contacted whom, when, how often and from where.

## tracker
also: trackers, tracking

Tools that websites and other companies use to record what you do online, such as cookies and scripts that follow your activity across sites.

## fingerprinting

Recognising your browser by the combination of its details, such as screen size and installed fonts, so a website can follow you without cookies.

## third-party cookies
also: third-party cookie

Small pieces of data saved in your browser by a company other than the website you opened, which that company can use to recognise you on other websites too.

## cross-site tracking

A company recording what you do on different websites and joining it into one record about you.

## content blocker
also: content blockers

A browser add-on, or a setting built into some browsers, that stops ads and trackers from loading on the pages you visit.

## ad personalisation
also: ad personalization

Choosing which ads to show you from what a company has recorded about you, such as your searches, the videos you watched and your general area.

## privacy dashboard

The page in an account's settings that shows what is saved about you, and where you can delete it or turn some of the saving off.
