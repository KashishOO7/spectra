
## encryption
also: encrypted, encrypt, unencrypted

Scrambling data so that it can be read only with the right key.

## full-disk encryption
also: FDE

Encryption of everything stored on a device, so that while it is turned off, nothing on it can be read without your password. Once you switch the device on and sign in, the data can be read again.

## booting
also: boot, boots

Starting up a computer and loading its operating system. A computer can also be started from a USB drive instead of its own disk.

## operating system
also: OS

The main software that runs a computer or phone, such as Windows, macOS, Android or iOS, and that every other app runs on.

## administrator account

An account on a computer that is allowed to change its settings and install programs.

## recovery key

A long code that unlocks an encrypted drive when it cannot be unlocked the usual way, such as after you forget the password or after a hardware change. If you lose both the password and the recovery key, the data cannot be recovered.

## biometrics
also: biometric

Unlocking with your face or your fingerprint instead of a code.

## NFC
also: near-field communication

A short-range radio that lets two devices exchange data when they are held about 4 centimetres apart or closer.

## Bluetooth tracker
also: Bluetooth trackers

A small tag, such as an AirTag, that people attach to their keys or bag so their phone can show where it is. Someone can also hide one in another person's bag or car to follow them.

## PIN

A code made only of numbers, used to unlock a phone, approve a card payment or sign in to an account. Each extra digit makes it harder to guess.

## patch
also: patched, patches, unpatched

An update that fixes a flaw in software you already have, often a security flaw.

## vulnerability
also: vulnerabilities

A weakness in software or a device that an attacker could use to make it do something it was never meant to do.

## firmware

The software stored inside a device's hardware to run the device itself, separate from the apps you install.

## open source

Software whose code is published under a licence that lets anyone read it, check what it does, change it and share it.

## factory reset

Putting a device back to the state it left the factory in, which deletes your data and settings.

## wake word

The word or name that makes a smart speaker or voice assistant start recording what you say next.

## digital key
also: digital keys

A car key kept in a phone app, which can lock, unlock and start the car the way a physical key does.
