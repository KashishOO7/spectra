
## title
Someone got into my account

## subtitle
You can't sign in, or you see things in it you did not do

## do-not
Do not try to get the account back on the device you think was broken into. Use another phone or computer.

## technical
- From a different device, go to the service's own sign-in page and reset the password with its forgot password link.
- Sign out every other session: look for "Active sessions", "Devices" or "Security" in the account's settings.
- Give every other account that uses the same password, or a similar one, its own new password.
- Turn on two-factor authentication if it was off, so a password alone is no longer enough to get in.
- Check the account's connected apps, and for an email account its forwarding rules too, and remove any you did not set up, because a password change may not cut them off.
- If it is your email account, also check its filters and its recovery email and phone number.
- If codes sent to your phone number can reset the account, ask your mobile provider whether they can put a PIN on your account with them, so it is harder for someone to trick them into giving your number away. Keep the PIN safe, because you may need it when you change phone or provider.

## plain
- Use a different device, your phone or a friend's computer, not the one you think was broken into.
- Go to the account's website or app, choose "Forgot password", and set a new password you have not used before.
- Once back in, look for "Active sessions" or "Devices" in Settings and sign out of everything.
- If you used the same password anywhere else, give each of those sites its own new password.
- Turn on two-factor authentication, so a password alone is no longer enough to get in.
- If it is your email account, turn off any forwarding rules or auto-replies you did not set up, and make sure its recovery email and phone number are still yours.
- Look for "Connected apps", "Third-party access" or "Apps with account access" in Settings and remove anything you do not recognise. A password change may not remove these.
- If codes sent to your phone number can reset the account, ask your mobile provider whether they can put a PIN on your account with them, so it is harder for someone to trick them into giving your number away. Keep the PIN safe, because you may need it when you change phone or provider.
