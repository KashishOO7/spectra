
## title
My phone or computer is lost or stolen

## subtitle
Your phone, laptop or tablet is missing

## do-not
Mark the phone or computer as lost now, from another device. It locks the next time it goes online, if Find My (Apple) or Find My Device (Android) was on before it went missing.

## technical
- iPhone, iPad or Mac: on another device, go to icloud.com/find, sign in, and mark the device as lost. It locks with your passcode the next time it is online.
- Android: on another device, go to android.com/find, sign in, and choose Mark as lost.
- Windows: sign in at account.microsoft.com and use Find my device.
- Erase it only if you do not expect it back. Erasing deletes everything on it, and on Android you can no longer find the device afterwards.
- Change the passwords for your email, bank and work accounts from a different device.
- Do not remove an iPhone, iPad or Mac from Find My or your Apple Account, even if you erase it. Apple says that turns off Activation Lock, the lock that stops a thief setting it up as their own.
- If it was a phone, call your mobile provider, ask them to suspend the SIM so nobody else receives your sign-in codes, and get a new SIM with the same number so your codes reach you again.
- Report it to the police with the serial number, which is on the original box or in your account's list of devices. An insurer may ask for the report number.
- If a message says your lost device has been found and asks for your passcode, a password or a code, it is a scam. Never give your passcode, passwords or codes to anyone who asks.

## plain
- On iPhone: go to icloud.com/find on another device, sign in, and mark your phone as lost to lock it.
- On Android: go to android.com/find on another device, sign in, and choose Mark as lost.
- For a laptop: icloud.com/find for a Mac, or account.microsoft.com and Find my device for Windows.
- Only erase it if you do not expect it back. On Android, once it is erased you cannot find it any more.
- Change your email and banking passwords right now from a different device.
- If it was a phone, call your mobile provider and ask them to suspend the SIM, so nobody else gets your sign-in codes. Then get a new SIM with the same number, so the codes come to you again.
- Do not remove an iPhone, iPad or Mac from Find My, even if you erase it, because that lets a thief set it up as their own.
- Report it to the police with the serial number, from the original box or your account's list of devices. An insurer may ask for the report number.
- If a message says your lost phone has been found, do not give the sender your passcode or any code.
